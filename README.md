# mois — global interspecific floral colour × moisture

**Scientific scope:** the association between photographed floral colour composition **among plant species** and moisture-related environment across the world. This repository is the destination for the cross-species global climate/soil analyses originally developed in [zuizui0223/fcp](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fcp). The separate **fcp** repository focuses on *within-species* photographed flower-colour variation, recurrent white–chromatic colour axes and geographic partitioning.

## The main ecological question

**Do photographed white versus coloured floral states covary with precipitation and atmospheric moisture among congeneric species, and how well does this relationship persist under photographic, spatial and alternative biological-measurement controls?**

The worldwide source contains **100,543 distinct original photo IDs** across **42,111 named taxa**, of which **45,953** received a classifiable visible flower hue and **54,590** remain unclassified. Main climate26 hue–environment contrasts involve **20,694 named species /1,934 qualifying genera**; soil36 uses a *different* complete-case population of **16,801 /1,665** for the comparable congeneric hue slopes. The global genus-equal white-colour coefficient for annual precipitation is **+2.255 percentage points per original source environmental SD** (within-scope 24-contrast maxT 95% [+0.319,+4.190]); atmospheric vapour pressure is **+3.952** ([+1.199,+6.704]). These are retrospective photographic *between-species* associations, not causal water-response, genotype-frequency or flower-pigmentation benefit/cost effects.

**Nonconfirmation matters:** strict-quality precipitation +1.229 pp (95% [−2.046,+4.504]), within-genus×coarse-cell +0.808 (95% [−3.556,+5.172]); the same-taxonomic-support paired spatial contrast has a simultaneous interval spanning zero. Independent NHM-described floral hue also fails to independently establish a positive geographic rainfall direction. Positive global and negative geographic/source checks belong in the same article.

## New research stream: seasonality of photographed white versus chromatic flowers

**From 16 October 2026, all new observation-month, seasonal white-frequency and monthly temperature / solar-radiation / moisture-environment inference is developed here in `mois`, not in `fcp`.** This is a *separate, as-yet-unconfirmed* spatiotemporal extension to the existing global interspecific moisture manuscript, not a retrospective replacement for its climate coefficients. See [the MOIS seasonal research plan](docs/MOIS_SEASONAL_WHITE_MOISTURE_RESEARCH_20261016.md) and [the source transfer ledger](docs/MOIS_SEASONAL_FCP_SOURCE_TRANSFER_20261016.json).

The relevant 2026-09-27 original FCP acquisition source contains **3,430,502 verified photo-ID opportunities in 1,000 outcome-blind selected taxa**, with all photo IDs carrying an original observation date. Those are **not 3.43 million flowering images**, and must not be pooled with the historical 100,543-photo global colour sample. The FCP original ROI-v4 method currently measured a **64-photo technical unit**; 27 were colour-classifiable, 4 contained suspect white/yellow floral-organ overlap, and flowering organ identity still needs independent validation. The standalone MOIS seasonal Python analysis scripts and synthetic tests are included here, but no observed monthly environmental/white-rate effect is established.

**Division of work:** `fcp` remains authoritative for original photo-ID source acquisition, blind segmentation, fixed multicolour method, and within-species colour polymorphism; `mois` owns post-measurement date/season processing, paired geographic-site temporal white fractions, climate-grid matching, sensitivity analyses and manuscript interpretation. Signed FCP output artifacts are upstream inputs, never copied over as new MOIS measurements.

### Latest measured seasonal evidence (17 October 2026)

The current genuine MOIS photo union contains **267,425 actual original photos / 129,795 automated four-hue outcomes**, of which **81,867 photographs have both observed-on day and a classifiable ROI hue**. The unadjusted photographic-white fraction peaks in local summer in both extra-tropical hemispheres, but this is only a *source-photo composition* description, not evidence that individual flowers or populations seasonally change their pigment.

A [new actual signed photo-season and climate-normal analysis](docs/MOIS_REAL_267425_SEASONAL_WHITE_CLIMATE_NORMAL_DIAGNOSTIC_20261017.md) uses the frozen original WorldClim site join on 248,312 of these photos and accepts only **61 same-taxon × 0.25°-cell × season-year groups** (47 taxa / 384 classified photos) with two independent observations in two seasons. Temperature (+5°C), precipitation (+50mm) and correctly unit-converted solar radiation (+50 W/m²) each have taxon-cluster-bootstrap intervals **including zero**. These are 1970–2000 monthly *normals*, **not** observed-year weather. The old WorldClim photo artifact mistakenly labelled solar-energy values `kJ/m²/day` as `W/m²`; the corrected forward sampler and this signed-result analysis apply the required **÷86.4** conversion while retaining the original artifact provenance. [Frozen reproducible numeric decision](results/mois_real_267425_season_climate_normal_decision_20261017.json) and [source photo season denominators](results/mois_real_267425_dated_photo_season_white_rates_20261017.csv).

**Not established:** photograph-specific independently confirmed open floral organs, observer-disjoint season colour comparisons at global scale, and actual month-of-observation temperature/radiation/moisture effects. The earlier historical interspecific annual water manuscript remains a separate estimand.

### Reproducible seasonal source and botanical quality gates

The original 2026-09-27 calendar metadata are now independently re-analysed in **MOIS**: [real seasonal candidate workflow](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mois/actions/runs/38909941099) confirmed **39 taxa and 268 same-cell/year two-season photographic-opportunity comparisons** under the preregistered in-project 24-observation-per-season cutoff. This is not a floral-colour or genetic polymorphism result. The input is a SHA-verified [FCP source-only artifact](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fcp/actions/runs/38901290387), not an extra copy of millions of photo pixels.

The [independent flower-organ review workflow](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mois/actions/runs/38910580515) produced a botanical review form for all **64 truly measured original FCP ROI photos**. All 64 remain *unreviewed* (0 independently confirmed open-petaloid flower outcomes); coloured ROIs, bracts, flowers and fruits are not conflated. [Twelve regression tests](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mois/actions/runs/38910483078) verify no tampered source identity, independent reviewers, third-party disagreement adjudication and no white-rate claim from unreviewed photos.

The executable methods and decision gates are in [the MOIS temporal-source/anthesis protocol](docs/MOIS_SEASONAL_SOURCE_OPPORTUNITY_AND_ANTHESIS_20261016.md). FCP continues upstream image acquisition/ROI and its own independent W/D polymorphism manuscript; seasonal white frequency, real month-climate comparisons and their ecological interpretation are MOIS-owned.

**New original-photo phenology quality evidence (16 October 2026):** [Read the 2025 real seasonal source QA](docs/MOIS_REAL_2025_INAT_PHENOLOGY_ORGAN_ROI_QC_20261016.md). Actual 2025 TerraClimate v1.1 temperature, solar radiation, precipitation, climate-water deficit and VPD were successfully joined to 64 original observer-disjoint FCP photo candidates across 21 grid cells, although only seven cells overlap between seasons. A public current iNaturalist annotation lookup recovers observation-level Flowering (two summer), Fruiting (four autumn) and No Evidence of Flowering (one summer/two autumn); absent annotations remain unknown. One fruiting-annotated observation contains a white **automated RGB ROI label**. The botanical identity of open petals/tepals is **still unreviewed**, and none of this constitutes a valid seasonal white-flower frequency or weather effect. The [source-locked aggregate cross-tab](results/mois_2025_asclepias_observation_phenology_qc/photo_roi_x_public_observation_phenology_64.csv) and [QC receipt](results/mois_2025_asclepias_observation_phenology_qc/source_observation_phenology_QC_receipt.json) preserve the separation and original source SHA.

**New source-observation bias result (17 October 2026):** [Original-photo 400-observation public Plant Phenology audit](docs/MOIS_REAL_400_SOURCE_OBSERVATION_PHENOLOGY_QUALITY_20261017.md) and [real iNaturalist source run #38989216871](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mois/actions/runs/38989216871) verified **all 400 original UUID/day/photo IDs**, but **280/400 (70%) have no interpretable current observation-level phenology annotation**. Current tag coverage varies profoundly across the five taxa (*Datura discolor* 78/80 vs *Achillea millefolium* 1/80), so using only tagged observations as open flowers would strongly alter species/site/season representation. The [10-group original species-season table](results/mois_400_live_observation_phenology_20261017/source_siteyear_season_annotation_coverage.csv) and [provenance receipt](results/mois_400_live_observation_phenology_20261017/verified_source_400_annotation_coverage_receipt.json) preserve the exact sample. These tags are current observation annotations, **not photo-specific verified petal/tepal anthesis**; all 400 human botanical reviews remain uncompleted, and no seasonal white-flower frequency or actual weather effect has been inferred.

## Verification in the new home

`python scripts/check_mois_migration.py` checks SHA-1 of Git blobs for the original FCP scripts, numeric ledgers, source figures and receipt JSONs; tests 1,280 +576 result rows and the negative source-sensitivity verdicts without requiring raw photo/model re-estimation. The GitHub Actions workflow `.github/workflows/mois-source-audit.yml` runs this source-integrity gate for `mois`. Optional figure regeneration: `python scripts/render_mois_global_moisture.py` after installing NumPy and matplotlib; immutable historical `figures/global_moisture_colour.svg` is not rewritten.

[Additional source-backed ecological sensitivity table](docs/MOIS_SUPPORTING_EVIDENCE_AND_NEGATIVE_GATES_20261014.md): original same-photo inter/within rainfall contrast, missing-colour MNAR, identical-species soil, unseen genera, distant geographic transfer, nonlinear model and independent botanical hue.

## Reproducibility and inference

The transferred analysis scripts and source-verifier scripts preserve the original model definitions and original source-archive checks. The original [source-verification run #38502660130](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fcp/actions/runs/38502660130) documents archived full source replay. The lightweight `mois` CI separately guards committed source tables and statements without pretending to redownload original photographs. Original source scripts may still require their historical raw ZIP/CSV environment and specific dependencies before a fresh end-to-end run.

**Not in scope:** prospective within-species white–chromatic test `W`, 1,499-species high-depth D and within-species 50-km local decomposition, which remain in [fcp](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fcp). Neither a photographic 46.95% white fraction nor the worldwide moisture coefficient proves a 50:50 evolutionary balance.
