# mois — global interspecific floral colour × moisture

**Scientific scope:** the association between photographed floral colour composition **among plant species** and moisture-related environment across the world. This repository is the destination for the cross-species global climate/soil analyses originally developed in [zuizui0223/fcp](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fcp). The separate **fcp** repository focuses on *within-species* photographed flower-colour variation, recurrent white–chromatic colour axes and geographic partitioning.

## The main ecological question

**Do photographed white versus coloured floral states covary with precipitation and atmospheric moisture among congeneric species, and how well does this relationship persist under photographic, spatial and alternative biological-measurement controls?**

The worldwide source contains **100,543 distinct original photo IDs** across **42,111 named taxa**, of which **45,953** received a classifiable visible flower hue and **54,590** remain unclassified. Main climate26 hue–environment contrasts involve **20,694 named species /1,934 qualifying genera**; soil36 uses a *different* complete-case population of **16,801 /1,665** for the comparable congeneric hue slopes. The global genus-equal white-colour coefficient for annual precipitation is **+2.255 percentage points per original source environmental SD** (within-scope 24-contrast maxT 95% [+0.319,+4.190]); atmospheric vapour pressure is **+3.952** ([+1.199,+6.704]). These are retrospective photographic *between-species* associations, not causal water-response, genotype-frequency or flower-pigmentation benefit/cost effects.

**Scope and precision:** The strict-photo (+1.229 pp, 95% [−2.046,+4.504]) and within-genus×coarse-cell (+0.808, [−3.556,+5.172]) rainfall point estimates remain **positive**, but their simultaneous intervals include zero. Smaller and different source populations make these nonconfirmations, **not evidence that the global association disappears**. The broad +2.255 pp/source SD result remains the interspecific ecological headline; source-quality and geographic-sensitivity boundaries belong in the supplement or a carefully bounded section, not as a second biological discovery.

## Photo-white near-parity and balancing-selection hypotheses (source-only)

The source-classifiable unique photographs were **21,574 white/24,379 coloured** (**46.95%/53.05%**) among 45,953 assigned four-hue labels, not a global species census. An exactly 50:50 *hypothetical all-photo* ratio requires **52.57% white among 54,590 unclassifiable photo opportunities**; neither the unclassified colours nor population morph genotypes are known. The source's original latitude-evenness null (`P=0.214`) and negative geographic compensation test (`P=0.997`) do **not** demonstrate an actively maintained ratio. **New exact original-ZIP replay:** among the 25,064 taxa with classifiable source photos, the species-equal white proportion is **46.82%**, whereas averaging across 5,369 named genera gives **49.89%** (1,999 whole-genus bootstrap 95% 48.74–50.98%). But 2,529 genera have only one classifiable species; restricting to ≥5 classifiable species/genus gives **46.63%**. Thus the apparent near-half depends strongly on the taxonomic weighting and source support, not an observed fitness equilibrium. See the [full-source aggregate result](results/mois_original_photo_species_genus_balance_20261014.json), [raw-ZIP replayer](scripts/analysis/mois_replay_species_genus_balance_20261014.py), [ecological interpretation](docs/MOIS_SOURCE_REPLAYED_SPECIES_GENUS_WHITE_BALANCE_20261014.md), [earlier aggregate-only arithmetic](results/mois_original_photo_white_balance_20261014.json) and [separate fitness-selection hypotheses](docs/MOIS_WHITE_CHROMATIC_BALANCE_AND_FITNESS_HYPOTHESES_20261014.md). This is a companion research question, **not a measured trait-fitness trade-off or a new primary-paper causal result**.

## New original-source analysis: genus clustering beneath near-half white photographs

**15 October 2026 source result:** Although the 5,369 sampled classifiable genera average **49.89%** photographed white at equal genus weight, the **20,725 taxa in 1,935 genera with ≥3 classifiable taxa** display strong **nominal-genus clustering**: genus R² is **34.38%** of taxon white-fraction variance, versus **9.33%** under 1,999 genus-size-preserving permutations (*P*=0.0005; exploratory). A rainfall/latitude/photographic-depth conditional permutation returns **9.62%** expected. Among 1,185 genera with ≥5 classifiable taxa, **23.38%** are ≥90% white or ≥90% coloured in their *mean photographed class*, versus **2.19%** under the global shuffled null. Original one-photo-per-taxon breadth data show **33.86%** genus R² vs **10.45%** expected, so the broad clustering is not simply repeated taxon-by-cell photos. These are photographic *species composition* associations, **not a phylogenetically calibrated measure of inherited flower colour, within-population frequency balance, or relative fitness**. [Full audit and ecological alternatives](docs/MOIS_GENUS_COLOUR_CLUSTERING_AND_BALANCE_20261015.md), [aggregate source result](results/mois_original_genus_clustering_20261015.json), [exact original-ZIP replayer](scripts/analysis/mois_replay_genus_clustering_20261015.py). Primary global white–water coefficients remain unchanged.

## Matched taxonomic-scale moisture finding (15 October 2026)

On **exactly the same 20,694 climate26-eligible species /1,934 genera**, the previously verified genus-centred photographed-white **BIO12 +2.255 pp/source SD** remains positive; on *those identical species*, the equally weighted **genus-mean** rainfall association is **+8.555** with ordinary whole-genus bootstrap 95% **[+6.220,+10.786]**. The paired genus-mean minus within-genus difference is **+6.300 [+3.666,+8.779]**. Annual vapour shows the same positive directions (**within +3.952; between +9.370**). The source models are separately adjusted for the same broad latitude and circular longitude geometry. Retaining genera with ≥5, ≥10 or ≥20 sampled species preserves both positive point directions, but changes sampling support. **Neither the larger genus-mean association nor the paired difference is a causal contribution of lineage turnover, natural selection or pigment resource costs**. Original source ZIP-replayed code: [within/between replayer](scripts/analysis/replay_mois_genus_between_within_moisture_20261015.py); [aggregate-only numerical receipt](results/mois_genus_between_within_moisture_20261015.json); [ecological interpretation](docs/MOIS_GENUS_TURNOVER_AND_CONGENERIC_MOISTURE_20261015.md). The GEB draft prioritizes the original signed within-genus result and reports this new matched taxonomic contrast as exploratory supporting ecology.

## Heterogeneous congeneric signs beneath the positive worldwide average (15 October 2026)

New original-source partial-covariance analysis on **the exact 20,694 climate26-qualified taxa /1,934 genera** finds that the adjusted worldwide photographed-white/BIO12 slope **+2.255 pp/source SD** is a *net of opposing genus-level contributions*, not a universal direction in every genus. On BIO12, **830 nominal genera contribute positively**, **719 negatively** and **385 have no variation in classified-photo white**. Among the 1,549 variable genera, **53.58%** contribute positively compared with a **49.96%** original within-genus label-permutation expectation (1,999 shuffles; exploratory one-sided `P=0.004`); water vapour gives **54.29% versus 49.66%** (`P=0.0005`). The largest 10% of genera by positive covariance contribute **60.28% of the positive BIO12 covariance mass**, not a share of causal effect. These are source-observed, post hoc descriptions; no source genotype, pollinator reproductive benefit, pigment fitness cost or general phylogenetic process is established. [Full ecological interpretation](docs/MOIS_CONGENERIC_MOISTURE_GENUS_SIGN_MOSAIC_20261015.md), [aggregate result](results/mois_congeneric_moisture_genus_sign_mosaic_20261015.json), [owner-held exact-source replayer](scripts/analysis/replay_mois_genus_sign_mosaic_20261015.py). Principal signed original moisture results remain unchanged.

## Ecological context: positive water–white slopes across sampled climate regimes (15 October 2026)

The **same 20,694 source-qualified species/1,934 genera** were split into equal groups of **967 genera** by each genus's original photo-source mean solar radiation, peak temperature (BIO5), and annual precipitation (BIO12), before refitting the **same genus-centred, genus-equal, broadly geographic-adjusted** photo-white/moisture slopes. Each half had a positive original-source **BIO12 white slope**: low/high solar **+2.076/+2.481**, cool/hot **+1.781/+2.485**, dry/wet **+3.381/+2.021 percentage points per original photo-site SD**. Annual atmospheric vapour slopes were also positive across all six halves. The between-half ordinary genus-bootstrap differences have 95% intervals spanning zero; **this does not identify an ecological interaction, pigment metabolic cost or protective fitness benefit**. This is one retrospective cohort analyzed in overlapping ways, not six independent biological replications. [Original-source replayer](scripts/analysis/mois_replay_climate_regime_moisture_20261015.py), [aggregate receipt](results/mois_climate_regime_moisture_20261015.json), [biological interpretation](docs/MOIS_CLIMATE_REGIME_WHITE_MOISTURE_20261015.md), GEB Supporting Information S2.8.

## Geographic white-photo associations: latitude, longitude and altitude (15 October 2026)

The full SHA256-verified original photo/environment tables were independently rejoined without recoding source colour labels. Of **100,348 geotagged original photo opportunities**, **45,879** had classifiable photographed hue. Raw classified-photo white fractions by absolute latitude (0–30°, 30–60°, 60–90°) were **47.08%, 46.79%, 49.00%** (the previously archived species-preserving latitude-evenness null **P=0.214**). Four 90° longitude sectors had white shares **46.31%, 46.28%, 45.80%, 49.49%**, with the highest observed share in 90–180°E; longitude is circular and not a monotone eastward explanatory variable. White fractions across original source all-photo altitude quartiles were **49.08%, 47.83%, 48.06%, 42.96%** from lowest to highest altitude.

On **the same 20,694 species /1,934 genera** as the macro source, equal-genus geographically modelled photographed-white share had a geography-only **−1.90 percentage points per +1,000 m elevation** (within-model geography-four maxT95 **[−3.49,−0.31]**), but after including atmospheric water vapour the elevation coefficient was **+1.01 [−1.71,+3.74]**, i.e. a different, inconclusive source-conditional association. Absolute latitude was **+0.48 points per +10° [−0.86,+1.83]** without climatic axes, but **+3.08 [+0.60,+5.57]** after adjusting for atmospheric vapour; photo-site vapour is strongly correlated with latitude (r≈−0.78). These post hoc designs **do not identify independent pigment photoprotection, climatic mediation, genetic morph frequencies or natural selection**, and their simultaneous intervals are **within each model only**. [Aggregate-only numerical result](results/mois_photo_colour_geography_synthesis_20261015.json), [full Supporting Information S2.9](docs/MOIS_GEB_SUPPORTING_INFORMATION_20261014.md). The owner-executed full-source Python analysis is retained separately from original licensed source data; original site and photo ZIPs are not distributed.
