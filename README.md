# mois — global interspecific floral colour × moisture

**Scientific scope:** the association between photographed floral colour composition **among plant species** and moisture-related environment across the world. This repository is the destination for the cross-species global climate/soil analyses originally developed in [zuizui0223/fcp](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fcp). The separate **fcp** repository focuses on *within-species* photographed flower-colour variation, recurrent white–chromatic colour axes and geographic partitioning.

## The main ecological question

**Do photographed white versus coloured floral states covary with precipitation and atmospheric moisture among congeneric species, and how well does this relationship persist under photographic, spatial and alternative biological-measurement controls?**

The worldwide source contains **100,543 distinct original photo IDs** across **42,111 named taxa**, of which **45,953** received a classifiable visible flower hue and **54,590** remain unclassified. Main climate26 hue–environment contrasts involve **20,694 named species /1,934 qualifying genera**; soil36 uses a *different* complete-case population of **16,801 /1,665** for the comparable congeneric hue slopes. The global genus-equal white-colour coefficient for annual precipitation is **+2.255 percentage points per original source environmental SD** (within-scope 24-contrast maxT 95% [+0.319,+4.190]); atmospheric vapour pressure is **+3.952** ([+1.199,+6.704]). These are retrospective photographic *between-species* associations, not causal water-response, genotype-frequency or flower-pigmentation benefit/cost effects.

**Scope and precision:** The strict-photo (+1.229 pp, 95% [−2.046,+4.504]) and within-genus×coarse-cell (+0.808, [−3.556,+5.172]) rainfall point estimates remain **positive**, but their simultaneous intervals include zero. Smaller and different source populations make these nonconfirmations, **not evidence that the global association disappears**. The broad +2.255 pp/source SD result remains the interspecific ecological headline; source-quality and geographic-sensitivity boundaries belong in the supplement or a carefully bounded section, not as a second biological discovery.

## Photo-white near-parity and balancing-selection hypotheses (source-only)

The source-classifiable unique photographs were **21,574 white/24,379 coloured** (**46.95%/53.05%**) among 45,953 assigned four-hue labels, not a global species census. An exactly 50:50 *hypothetical all-photo* ratio requires **52.57% white among 54,590 unclassifiable photo opportunities**; neither the unclassified colours nor population morph genotypes are known. The source's original latitude-evenness null (`P=0.214`) and negative geographic compensation test (`P=0.997`) do **not** demonstrate an actively maintained ratio. **New exact original-ZIP replay:** among the 25,064 taxa with classifiable source photos, the species-equal white proportion is **46.82%**, whereas averaging across 5,369 named genera gives **49.89%** (1,999 whole-genus bootstrap 95% 48.74–50.98%). But 2,529 genera have only one classifiable species; restricting to ≥5 classifiable species/genus gives **46.63%**. Thus the apparent near-half depends strongly on the taxonomic weighting and source support, not an observed fitness equilibrium. See the [full-source aggregate result](results/mois_original_photo_species_genus_balance_20261014.json), [raw-ZIP replayer](scripts/analysis/mois_replay_species_genus_balance_20261014.py), [ecological interpretation](docs/MOIS_SOURCE_REPLAYED_SPECIES_GENUS_WHITE_BALANCE_20261014.md), [earlier aggregate-only arithmetic](results/mois_original_photo_white_balance_20261014.json) and [separate fitness-selection hypotheses](docs/MOIS_WHITE_CHROMATIC_BALANCE_AND_FITNESS_HYPOTHESES_20261014.md). This is a companion research question, **not a measured trait-fitness trade-off or a new primary-paper causal result**.

## New original-source analysis: genus clustering beneath near-half white photographs

**15 October 2026 source result:** Although the 5,369 sampled classifiable genera average **49.89%** photographed white at equal genus weight, the **20,725 taxa in 1,935 genera with ≥3 classifiable taxa** display strong **nominal-genus clustering**: genus R² is **34.38%** of taxon white-fraction variance, versus **9.33%** under 1,999 genus-size-preserving permutations (*P*=0.0005; exploratory). A rainfall/latitude/photographic-depth conditional permutation returns **9.62%** expected. Among 1,185 genera with ≥5 classifiable taxa, **23.38%** are ≥90% white or ≥90% coloured in their *mean photographed class*, versus **2.19%** under the global shuffled null. Original one-photo-per-taxon breadth data show **33.86%** genus R² vs **10.45%** expected, so the broad clustering is not simply repeated taxon-by-cell photos. These are photographic *species composition* associations, **not a phylogenetically calibrated measure of inherited flower colour, within-population frequency balance, or relative fitness**. ## Matched taxonomic-scale moisture finding (15 October 2026)

On **exactly the same 20,694 climate26-eligible species /1,934 genera**, the previously verified genus-centred photographed-white **BIO12 +2.255 pp/source SD** remains positive; on *those identical species*, the equally weighted **genus-mean** rainfall association is **+8.555** with ordinary whole-genus bootstrap 95% **[+6.220,+10.786]**. The paired genus-mean minus within-genus difference is **+6.300 [+3.666,+8.779]**. Annual vapour shows the same positive directions (**within +3.952; between +9.370**). The source models are separately adjusted for the same broad latitude and circular longitude geometry. Retaining genera with ≥5, ≥10 or ≥20 sampled species preserves both positive point directions, but changes sampling support. **Neither the larger genus-mean association nor the paired difference is a causal contribution of lineage turnover, natural selection or pigment resource costs**. ## Heterogeneous congeneric signs beneath the positive worldwide average (15 October 2026)

New original-source partial-covariance analysis on **the exact 20,694 climate26-qualified taxa /1,934 genera** finds that the adjusted worldwide photographed-white/BIO12 slope **+2.255 pp/source SD** is a *net of opposing genus-level contributions*, not a universal direction in every genus. On BIO12, **830 nominal genera contribute positively**, **719 negatively** and **385 have no variation in classified-photo white**. Among the 1,549 variable genera, **53.58%** contribute positively compared with a **49.96%** original within-genus label-permutation expectation (1,999 shuffles; exploratory one-sided `P=0.004`); water vapour gives **54.29% versus 49.66%** (`P=0.0005`). The largest 10% of genera by positive covariance contribute **60.28% of the positive BIO12 covariance mass**, not a share of causal effect. These are source-observed, post hoc descriptions; no source genotype, pollinator reproductive benefit, pigment fitness cost or general phylogenetic process is established. ## Ecological context: positive water–white slopes across sampled climate regimes (15 October 2026)

The **same 20,694 source-qualified species/1,934 genera** were split into equal groups of **967 genera** by each genus's original photo-source mean solar radiation, peak temperature (BIO5), and annual precipitation (BIO12), before refitting the **same genus-centred, genus-equal, broadly geographic-adjusted** photo-white/moisture slopes. Each half had a positive original-source **BIO12 white slope**: low/high solar **+2.076/+2.481**, cool/hot **+1.781/+2.485**, dry/wet **+3.381/+2.021 percentage points per original photo-site SD**. Annual atmospheric vapour slopes were also positive across all six halves. The between-half ordinary genus-bootstrap differences have 95% intervals spanning zero; **this does not identify an ecological interaction, pigment metabolic cost or protective fitness benefit**. This is one retrospective cohort analyzed in overlapping ways, not six independent biological replications. ## Geographic white-photo associations: latitude, longitude and altitude (15 October 2026)

The full SHA256-verified original photo/environment tables were independently rejoined without recoding source colour labels. Of **100,348 geotagged original photo opportunities**, **45,879** had classifiable photographed hue. Raw classified-photo white fractions by absolute latitude (0–30°, 30–60°, 60–90°) were **47.08%, 46.79%, 49.00%** (the previously archived species-preserving latitude-evenness null **P=0.214**). Four 90° longitude sectors had white shares **46.31%, 46.28%, 45.80%, 49.49%**, with the highest observed share in 90–180°E; longitude is circular and not a monotone eastward explanatory variable. White fractions across original source all-photo altitude quartiles were **49.08%, 47.83%, 48.06%, 42.96%** from lowest to highest altitude.

On **the same 20,694 species /1,934 genera** as the macro source, equal-genus geographically modelled photographed-white share had a geography-only **−1.90 percentage points per +1,000 m elevation** (within-model geography-four maxT95 **[−3.49,−0.31]**), but after including atmospheric water vapour the elevation coefficient was **+1.01 [−1.71,+3.74]**, i.e. a different, inconclusive source-conditional association. Absolute latitude was **+0.48 points per +10° [−0.86,+1.83]** without climatic axes, but **+3.08 [+0.60,+5.57]** after adjusting for atmospheric vapour; photo-site vapour is strongly correlated with latitude (r≈−0.78). These post hoc designs **do not identify independent pigment photoprotection, climatic mediation, genetic morph frequencies or natural selection**, and their simultaneous intervals are **within each model only**. [Aggregate-only numerical result](results/mois_photo_colour_geography_synthesis_20261015.json), [full Supporting Information S2.9](docs/MOIS_GEB_SUPPORTING_INFORMATION_20261014.md). ## Why elevation and latitude change after adding water-vapour predictors

A new exact **source-genus-centred omitted-variable check** on the original **20,694 species/1,934 genera** explains the apparent altitude coefficient reversal without invoking causal pigment adaptation. In the broad geography-only photographic-white regression, elevation was **−1.903 percentage points per +1,000 m**; after including atmospheric vapour it became **+1.011**, a same-species paired increase of **+2.914 pp [ordinary genus-bootstrap 95% +1.110,+4.759]**. Absolute-latitude +10° shifted from **+0.483 to +3.083**, an increase of **+2.600 [+0.995,+4.233]**. Exact weighted-regression algebra reproduced the coefficient shifts from the vapour–geography projection, with **74.53%** of the source genus-centred vapour variation predicted by the other geographic covariates (VIF **3.927**). With rainfall included among geography predictors, vapour overlap rose to **78.57%** (VIF **4.667**); the altitude and latitude coefficient shifts remained in the same direction. This is an **observational conditioning/collinearity interpretation, not climate-mediated fitness selection**. ## Joint precipitation and atmospheric-vapour check on identical source taxa (15 October 2026)

A new **source-SHA256-replayed, exact-same-species** follow-up on the original **20,694 species/1,934 genera** jointly considers the two principal moisture-related axes. The separate original broad congeneric source white associations remain **rainfall +2.255 pp/source SD** and **mean atmospheric vapour +3.952** (original world 24-contrast maxT intervals remain primary). When both axes and altitude are fitted with the same broad geography, positive water-axis coefficients become **rainfall +1.383** (selected two-contrast maxT95 **[−0.167,+2.934]**) and **vapour +3.771 [−0.163,+7.704]**; neither individual partial association is sufficiently precise under that specification. Reparameterizing the same two environmental predictors into a **common standardized moisture-related direction** `(z_rain+z_vapour)/sqrt(2)` and a rain-minus-vapour contrast preserves the full fitted model. Its common-direction white slope remains **+3.644 pp [within-model two-contrast maxT95 +1.137,+6.152]**, while the difference-axis interval includes zero. Thus the source evidence is consistent with a **shared moisture-related floristic gradient**, not separately established water-axis mechanisms, pigment metabolic costs or water-driven natural selection. This is **exploratory original-source reanalysis, not independent biological replication**. ## Matched biogeography, climate, soil, elevation and true-terrain audit (15 October 2026)

The original SHA256-verified photo and environment source archives were reopened and analysed at fixed soil36 eligibility. The historical worldwide source cohort was **100,543 unique photo opportunities /45,953 classifiable**; a congeneric main climate26 population has **20,694 species/1,934 genera**, while its soil36-complete cohort has **16,801 species/1,665 genera** and 31,925 soil-complete classifiable photos. The original positive soil36 white/BIO12 point coefficient **+2.256937 pp/source soil36 photo-site SD** was independently reproduced.

**A reproducible predictive ecological difference:** Whole-genus blocked fivefold out-of-genus evaluation on exactly those **16,801 soil-complete species**, equal-genus species-white-photo fraction squared error and fixed ridge regularisation shows **water-related climate adds +0.002355** to the geography-only prediction gain (heldout-genus bootstrap 95% **[+0.001125,+0.003608]**). Adding five soil descriptors to the full climate/elevation model **reduces** this fixed-source prediction improvement by **−0.000435 [−0.000700,−0.000171]**; adding ten soil descriptors reduces it by **−0.000620**. An additional full-block fixed-ridge comparison on the **same 16,801 species**, using **all 26 original climate predictors plus geography**, obtained heldout photo-white error **0.228133**; soil5 addition increased error to **0.228537** (soil incremental gain **−0.000404**), soil10 to **0.228661** (**−0.000528**, genus-bootstrap 95% **[−0.000873,−0.000173]**). [All26 source replayer](scripts/analysis/mois_full26_soil10_transfer_20261015.py) · [all26/soil5/soil10 receipt](results/mois_full26_climate_soil10_transfer_20261015.json). By contrast, the original soil **available-water proxy** has an exploratory *in-sample* conditional coefficient **+1.907** (selected seven-axis within-model maxT95 **[+0.199,+3.615]**)—association is not necessarily transferable prediction or causal water retention. Raw sand/silt/clay are nearly a closed composition, with unsafe unpenalized matrix conditioning. Soil36 photo completion varies from **44.6% in the lowest to 87.7% in the highest source elevation quartile**, raising substantial geographical sample-selection questions.

**Biogeography is heterogeneous, not universally portable:** In source longitude 90° holdouts **excluding every test-region genus from training**, moisture gain was positive in three geographical sectors, but **−0.000233 in 0–90°E**. A supplemental Natural Earth 110m country-polygon *approximate continent* overlay of the climate26 source species leaves **3,158/20,694 (15.3%) unmapped**, especially relevant for islands/coastal sampling; it is neither Wallace's biogeographical realms nor a plant flora census.

**Important unfinished terrain distinction:** Photo-site `wc_elevation_m` is **point altitude, not geomorphometry**. The original FCP environmental ZIP does **not** include 3×3 DEM neighbourhoods, slope, slope aspect, topographic position (TPI), terrain ruggedness (TRI) or hydrological wetness indices; **no actual source colour–terrain effect has yet been estimated**. A new separately projected-GIS DEM extraction script with synthetic flat, inclined, ridge and valley accuracy gates supports future tests only when independent raster DEM tiles and author-approved site data are available. The existing rainfall/vapour macro result remains the main paper's positive ecology rather than a newly verified pigment cost–benefit or causal selection effect.

## Soil-block transfer: ridge-penalty and genus-fold robustness (15 October 2026)

An additional original **SHA256-verified photo/site ZIP** reanalysis held **16,801 soil36-classifiable named species /1,665 genera** fixed and repeated the same photo-white-fraction genus-heldout prediction using *all 26 original climate variables and broad geography*, then soil5 or all soil10. Across **8 ridge penalties ×4 distinct full-genus fivefold assignments =32 correlated sensitivity settings**, adding soil5 increased prediction error in **31/32**, adding soil10 increased it in **32/32**. The one soil5 exception used very strong ridge α=1 with a tiny **+0.000012 heldout prediction improvement**. At α=0.01 every genus-fold assignment had negative soil increments, but some ordinary whole-genus bootstrap 95% intervals crossed zero. This indicates **little stable incremental transfer information from these source soil layers** under the tested fixed models, **not a demonstration that soil influences cannot affect flower colour or fitness**. Retrospective 32-way sensitivity is not 32 independent biological experiments, an externally validated hyperparameter-tuning procedure or a way to resolve nonrandom soil/photo missingness.

## Matched soil versus water climate block comparison (15 October 2026)

Using **two SHA256-verified original FCP ZIPs**, an independent genus-heldout replayer compared environmental predictor groups on the **same 16,801 soil36-complete taxa /1,665 genera**, with equal-genus photo-white squared loss and training-only ridge standardization.

**Soil has some photographic predictive association, but not much unique prediction after climate:** relative to source geography-only, **soil5** lowers heldout loss by **+0.001032** at ridge α=0.01 (ordinary genus-bootstrap 95% **[−0.000012,+0.001997]**, overlapping zero). The **annual rainfall plus atmospheric vapour** block lowers loss by **+0.002355 [+0.001134,+0.003574]**, outperforming soil5 by **+0.001323 [+0.000436,+0.002214]** on the **identical target species**. Yet adding rainfall/vapour **after the other 24 correlated climate variables** gains only **+0.000040 [−0.000141,+0.000226]**: the shared water-related ecological gradient is not an independently identified physiological rainfall/vapour mechanism. Adding soil5/soil10 to full26 climate still **worsens** prediction under the same design (−0.000404 and −0.000528), consistent with the larger prior ridge/fold robustness audit. Results remain directionally consistent at ridge α=0.001, 0.01 and 0.1.

New [matched crossdomain numerical receipt](results/mois_soil_crossdomain_block_audit_20261015.json), [original-source replay code](scripts/analysis/mois_soil_vs_climate_blocks_20261015.py), [full ecological audit](docs/MOIS_BIOGEOGRAPHY_CLIMATE_SOIL_ELEVATION_TERRAIN_AUDIT_20261015.md) and [GEB Supporting Information S2.14](docs/MOIS_GEB_SUPPORTING_INFORMATION_20261014.md) distinguish **standalone predictive association** from **conditional incremental predictive information**. The historic photo sampling, soil completeness, broad coordinates and real terrain limitations remain; source point elevation is **not** a measured slope/aspect/TPI/TRI, and neither soil nor climate prediction measures pigment fitness.

## Does soil become predictive in dry, hot, mountain or high-latitude floras? (15 October 2026)

Independently rereading both original SHA256-verified FCP photo/environment ZIPs, we reproduced the **same 16,801 soil36-qualified species/1,665 genera** and the earlier genus-heldout full-climate predictions from independently reimplemented ridge fits: original fixed source photo-white squared errors were geography **0.229810064**, rainfall+vapour **0.227455352**, full climate26 **0.228132885**, full climate26+soil5 **0.228537347**, and full climate26+soil10 **0.228661099**. The soil5-versus-climate26 source increment was **−0.000404461**, ordinary 1,999-genus bootstrap95 **[−0.000674388,−0.000129229]**.

After separately splitting the source taxa at their observed annual precipitation (**834mm**), peak temperature (**27.528°C**), elevation (**547.5m**) and absolute latitude (**32.277°**) medians, **all eight subsets showed a negative soil5-after-full26 predictive increment**. The eight subset point estimates are not eight independent statistical tests and differ in taxonomic support. Crucially, **all eight *high-minus-low* differences** across these 4 original-species environment splits × 2 predictive targets (water2 gain vs geography and soil5 increment vs full26) had selected-eight-family maxT95 intervals **including zero**. Hence there is no confirmed climate-, elevation- or latitude-specific interaction explaining the soil block failure; this is a **source-conditional post hoc predictive diagnostic, not an exclusion of soil biology**.

For genuinely independent terrain variables, the peer-reviewed EarthEnv/Amatulli *et al.* [multi-grain global topography](https://www.earthenv.org/topography) (1/5/10/50/100km slope, aspect, TPI, TRI and landform classes; [PANGAEA source DOI](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.867115), CC BY 3.0) is a documented candidate **not yet downloaded or linked to original photographic sites**. Elevation alone is not a terrain morphology measurement.
