## Scientific scope in one sentence

> Existing worldwide source-measured floral photo hues show a moisture-associated broad-scale pattern, but taxonomic weighting, geographical support, landscape contrasts and season-specific photographed assemblages change what that relationship can mean.

**Recommended submission target:** Global Ecology and Biogeography, **Research Article**, provisionally. Its current guidelines support a macroecological/assemblage question, typically ~5,000 text words and 6–8 figures/tables, and require a structured abstract **Aim, Location, Time period, Major taxa studied, Methods, Results, Main conclusions** with <=300 words. It uses double-anonymous review. Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14668238/homepage/forauthors.html (checked 27 Oct 2026). This is a recommendation, not an acceptance claim.

## Claims ready for a source-backed manuscript now

1. **Photo composition, N=350,399 actual original image attempts:** 164,297 four-hue classifiable, white photo fraction 47.4908%, taxon-equal 48.1891%, genus-equal 51.4631%. Species/genus source weighting demonstrably matters. No world-wide 50:50 biological pigment equilibrium.
2. **Worldwide annual precipitation/vapour association, N=309,439 actual original images:** positive white-photo association with annual BIO12 +1.9884 pp/source SD [0.5525,3.4244] and vapour +3.1606 [1.0420,5.2792], 999 whole-genus bootstrap / 24-hue×environment simultaneous intervals *within each frozen comparison*. Genus×coarse-geography conditional intervals span zero, therefore no universal local response.
3. **Taxonomic/geographic photographic composition:** original identical 20,694 taxon/1,934 genus global source had among-genus precipitation slope > within-genus; 350,399 core vs taxon-enrichment arm +3.7847 pp raw white difference becomes +0.635 pp with 10° genus/geography support (different estimands). Source taxonomic weighting, not taxonomic evolution alone.
4. **Elevation/island contrasts:** 44 nearby neighbourhoods high vs low elevation -5.085 pp white [−8.785,−1.386]; 102 climate/elevation-matched island–mainland pairs +1.396 [−1.553,4.345] (exploratory nominal intervals). No global island-white law or pollinator causal attribution.
5. **Seasonal photo-composition accounting, N=350,399 actual photo attempts:** Southern extratropical summer minus winter +10.49624 pp photographed white, with +8.03618 pp relative photo frequency of taxa photographed in both seasons. Under same taxon/cell/season-year ≥2 distinct source observations per season there are only 2 matched groups per hemisphere. This is source photo composition, not pigment plasticity.
6. **Modern source technical validation, N=1,181,146 authentic original-photo attempts:** 451,643 strict nine-component ROIs, 729,503 technical unknown. Source-frame colour support 30,048 /42,111 nominal taxa after exact cross-arm photo+observation dedup. 131,072 bulk source photo geographies and observation days were restored in the corrected v2; regional strict9 availability differs markedly. This is a data-quality guardrail; NO climate/ecological slope for the 1.18m strict9 subset has been fit.

## Main manuscript / SI division

**Keep in the main paper:** Figure 1 photo composition/weighting, Figure 2 moisture global–geography contrast, Figure 3 source taxonomic structure, Figure 4 elevation–island matched support, Figure 5 exact seasonal photographic composition. One compact Table 1 disambiguates five partially overlapping analytical source populations.

**Place in SI, not separate manuscripts now:** exploratory 13 soil/environment covariates and old genus R²; class-specific 4-hue wet/dry solar analyses whose released CSV needs numerical figure reconciliation; source-bias and matching-caliper sensitivities; photo IDs/sample receipts, cross-arm 1.18m strict9 technical missingness and geography restoration; 512 existing technical unknown photo diagnostics and 128 still-blank human flower-organ review sample.

**Do not add:** pigment production costs, fitness, pollinator shortage maps, UV reflectance, independent phylogenies, within-individual seasonal changes, validated island nativeness, full-93m source prevalence or actual-observation-month weather coefficients. These are scientifically different estimands, currently unmeasured.
