# Mois — iNaturalist flowering annotations and observation-bias controls

**16 October 2026. Prospective protocol amendment, not a newly observed biological result.** Target: one global interspecific **flower-colour** paper. Existing historical estimates are unchanged. The separately frozen FCP within-species study is unaffected.

## Platform reality and denominator

iNaturalist Flowers and Fruits (formerly Plant Phenology) uses annotation term ID **12**: **13 = Flowers**, **15 = Flower Buds**, **14 = Fruits or Seeds**, **21 = No Flowers or Fruits**. Compatible stages may coexist on one observation. The official [definitions](https://www.inaturalist.org/pages/annotationvalues) require open, visible flowers for Flowers. The [official iNaturalist observation filter for Flowers](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations?term_id=12&term_value_id=13) exists.

**Annotation is linked to the observation, NOT proof that a particular attached photo has a classifiable petal ROI.** The [dated iNaturalist Open Dataset](https://github.com/inaturalist/inaturalist-open-data/blob/main/Metadata/README.md) has observations, observers, photos and taxa in four tables, **without flowering annotations**. Thus the independently counted 93,209,351 source photo records (42,111 target taxa) are plant photographic *opportunities*, **not 93M confirmed flower photographs**. The global number with a Flowers annotation is not yet measured. New annotations may postdate the September 27 original photo snapshot; preserve the annotation lookup's separate date.

## Fixed study path

1. Preserve the already designed outcome-blind, <=40 distinct observation/species, site-BIO12 × geographic Mois photo sample, with per-observation and per-photo selection probabilities. Do **not** drop unannotated observations at source sampling and then claim worldwide unbiased floral prevalence.
2. Obtain a dated, properly authorized, **bounded** iNaturalist observation API/export result for the selected UUIDs (no 1M+ API crawl). An empty annotations array on a verified observation means **NO_ANNOTATION**, *not* no flower. A missing/unretrieved observation is **NOT_VERIFIED**. A positive Flowers (13) value is high-confidence observation-level flower evidence but the sampled individual photo still needs ROI-v4. Buds-only, fruits-only and explicit no-flowers annotations are distinct; a measured flower in explicit no-flower evidence is a contradiction for blinded review.
3. Use the ROI-classifiable selected photographs as the **primary conditional-photo-colour estimand**. Restrict to both Flowers-annotated and ROI-classifiable observations as a clearly labelled **secondary high-precision subset**. Never misinterpret this strict subset as a probability sample of all flowers on Earth.
4. Report geographic/climate, genus, observer, observed-month/year and annotation-availability distribution in *both* subsets. Compare same-source, same-13-variable species-mean, genus-between and genus-within climate regressions, while reporting sample denominators and the increase in selection caused by filtering on annotation and successful image segmentation.
5. Report number of selected observations, observer concentration (largest 1% share, design-weighted effective number), months by location/season, geographic precipitation bins, missing annotations and ROI classifiability by annotation status. In addition to observation/sample inclusion weights, use observer-disjoint or observer-balanced sensitivity where support permits. Missing-not-at-random flower visibility can persist even after adjustment.

**Essential caveat:** observer behavior, flower conspicuousness, uploaded photo selection, licensing and platform geography are not a random sample of wild plant populations. Observation correction does not identify the flower-colour frequency of plants never submitted to iNaturalist. Phenology annotations themselves have selection bias; a no-annotation observation is not biologically nonflowering.

## Implemented offline reproducibility path

- Source importer: scripts/acquisition/mois_annotation_overlay_from_inat_export_20261016.py. It uses a private, SHA256-pinned locally obtained observation JSONL with UUID, complete annotations list and positive vote evidence; does not contact the provider itself. It exports all selected UUIDs with checked, not-checked or explicit annotated status and the separately pinned annotation lookup date.
- Observer and phenology audit: scripts/analysis/mois_phenology_observer_bias_audit_20261016.py. Reads only the completed and SHA-verified Mois image-ROI terminal join and independent private observation annotation overlay. Reports annotated Flowers versus unverified and true no-evidence separately, as well as weighted ROI classifiability/white shares, observer concentration, month distribution and contradictory annotations.
- Tests: tests/test_mois_annotation_overlay_from_inat_export_20261016.py and tests/test_mois_phenology_observer_bias_audit_20261016.py. CI uses only synthetic observations; it is not a new phenology prevalence measurement.

The importer accepts CLI arguments --private-selected-sample, --authorized-observations-jsonl, --authorized-observations-sha256, --annotation-snapshot-date, --out-private-overlay and --out-private-receipt. After actual complete ROI-v4 sample photo collection, the bias audit accepts --private-joined-sample, --sample-collection-receipt, --observation-annotation-overlay, --annotation-overlay-sha256, --out-private-aggregates and --out-public-aggregate. Raw observer IDs, image IDs, coordinates and lookup data stay **restricted**.

**Not yet done:** the full source photo-ID freezer, complete authorized Flowers annotation lookups for the selected observations, real photo-ROI measurements and the new 13-variable ecological refits. Old original white–BIO12 and 13-variable coefficients remain valid only for their historical classified-photo observational cohort.


## Practical photo measurement ordering: confirmed flowering first, full sample preserved

The [phenology-prioritized Mois ROI-v4 scheduler](../scripts/acquisition/mois_prioritize_sampled_flowers_20261016.py) takes the already source-admitted, SHA-sealed **Mois-only** ROI input packets and an **independently SHA-pinned, every-selected-observation annotation overlay**. It preserves the **exact same selected photos, original observation/photo IDs, selection probabilities, taxon/genus coverage and private metadata join**. It changes only the order of original image evaluation inside each of 128 partitions and emits an explicit **global 64-photo batch work order**.

Priority classes: first **Flowers (13)**, then checked-but-unannotated and not-verified observations (which may contain flowers), followed by Buds-only, Fruits-only, and explicitly No Flowers/Fruits. Buds-only is not itself evidence of an open corolla. The secondary ordering is operational only; it is **not** a final flower phenotype, not a claim of image-only ROI success and cannot be used for photo-subset-only model estimation. The original complete sample remains the only acceptable production terminal for the main observational photo-colour estimand. Even a completed early Flowers-only queue is merely an **annotated diagnostic subset** with its own different annotation-selection process.

Offline usage after source admission and annotation lookup: run scripts/acquisition/mois_prioritize_sampled_flowers_20261016.py with --original-sample-roi-packets, --private-observation-annotation-overlay, --annotation-overlay-sha256, and --out-private-prioritized-packets. Feed the resealed 128 blind packets to the existing Mois-only original ROI-v4 executor, following private_phenology_roi_work_order.csv. Do NOT pass them to the full 93M-photo FCP study firewall. Completion, duplicate/source SHA checks, provider rate coordination and mandatory no-omission reassembly remain unchanged. The [regression tests](../tests/test_mois_prioritize_sampled_flowers_20261016.py) verify the unchanged selected-photo universe and class-priority ordering using synthetic data only.


## New real API evidence: angiosperm annotation availability is observation-season dependent

The platform-wide availability of Flowers annotations is now **actually measured on a separate, explicitly external source population**, rather than merely a proposed test. [Actual 35-query Angiospermae-only iNaturalist public API count receipt](../results/mois_real_inat_angiosperm_phenology_annotation_count_pilot_20261016.json) (verified original [GitHub Actions #38844098039](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mois/actions/runs/38844098039)) has **26,069,247 photographed flowering-plant observations in observed year 2025**, of which **2,550,231 (9.78%)** have a Flowers/Fruits annotation and **1,615,314 (6.20%)** have positive Flowers annotation. The public without-term-12 filter returns **23,518,398 (90.21%)**, not an observation class of confirmed nonflowering plants. Across Northern East Asian local spring/summer, Southern Australian local spring/summer, and two calendar tropical South American periods, the positive Flowers annotation rate ranges from approximately **4.15% to 17.80%**. Fixed geographical and seasonal observation cohort composition and observer annotation habits are intertwined; no single causal bias coefficient is identified from these counts. The separately retained [broad Plantae comparison](../results/mois_real_inat_photo_phenology_annotation_count_pilot_20261016.json) includes nonflowering plant lineages and uses a different denominator.

**Nothing here bypasses the still missing original 42,111-taxon iNaturalist 93M photo-ID admission or the per-observation selected UUID annotation overlay.** The current Angiospermae result is an externally sourced current-platform **count audit**, not a joined sample of original FCP photographs, and it cannot estimate actual petal ROI success or reproduce the original 13-variable flower-white climatic coefficient. Use it to justify the need for **two-stage independently joined annotation/ROI evidence** and an explicitly labelled Flowers-restricted sensitivity, not as an image-level negative flowering label or propensity weight for unknown original observations. All API count filters are non-atomic; the global term-12 any/without source difference of 618 observations was retained, not corrected by fabrication.
