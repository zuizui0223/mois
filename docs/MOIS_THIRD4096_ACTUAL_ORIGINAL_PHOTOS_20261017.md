# MOIS third original-photo measurement wave: no duplicate pixels or FCP phenotype hypotheses

The main objective remains **maximizing genuine classified photo hues**, then refitting the original **100,543-photo MOIS global white–moisture model**. Source photographs may overlap across `fcp` and `mois`, but the FCP study's within-species polymorphism D/W/50 km diversity results must not be imported as MOIS responses.

The original fixed existing study has **248,312 unique photo IDs**, with **120,807 original ROI-v4 four-class photographed hue assignments**. New *first* source 4,096 authentic photo-ID cohort [38994341776](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mois/actions/runs/38994341776) produced exactly 1,576 new hue assignments, now materialized as 252,408 photos/122,383 classified. A second 4,096-ID authentic photo-source cohort was selected outside the original 106,376-photo screen and all existing 248,312 photo IDs and is being measured in [38995240689](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mois/actions/runs/38995240689).

This PR launches a **third real original-photo measurement wave** from the same previously authenticated 2026-09-27 **3,430,502-source-photo pool**. The original candidate IDs are selected without reading photographic colour, flowering status, environmental covariates or previous ROI outcomes. They are excluded from the old 248,312, all first-106,376-screen photos (hence also the first measured 4,096), and the *entire second selected 4,096 photo-ID cohort*. This enforces source-photo nonoverlap of the three MOIS measurement waves and preserves their original licensed image links.

The third source ledger retains the original 3,315,910 unmeasured IDs outside the first 106,376-photo screen and subtracts the second reserved 4,096, leaving 3,311,814 source IDs outside that screen before the third-wave selection. These are **candidate source photo IDs**, not classified hues.

Image inference uses the **same original FCP ROI-v4/YOLO/EfficientSAM and nine-palette→four-hue method** as the 100,543-photo MOIS classifier family, via the already-tested `scripts/analysis/mois_measure_original_new_photos_roi_v4.py`. There are 16 × 256 real image pixel jobs with at most two simultaneous original source downloaders. All 4,096 original source IDs must produce a terminal outcome, including the technical unclassifiable outcomes; no source photos are replaced based on phenotype.

GitHub Actions [third-wave workflow](../.github/workflows/mois-original-third4096-real-ROI.yml) authenticates the original full-photo SHA256 and the previous wave2 selected-photo SHA256, independently reconstructs the 4,096 original photo IDs and per-job sources, and saves both signed source manifests and original ROI terminal artifacts.

**No third-wave flower-colour totals are claimed before original image inference finishes.** The final MOIS water analysis is genus-equal, with six prespecified humidity/temperature/rainfall/solar axes, four hue responses, 999 genus bootstrap resamples, and maxT adjusted simultaneous CIs. It should be rerun after the completed third-wave photograph source is integrated, without overwriting earlier 100k, 248k, 252k, or 256k historical receipts.
