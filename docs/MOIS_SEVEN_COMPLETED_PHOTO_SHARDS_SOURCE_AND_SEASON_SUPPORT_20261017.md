The joined original source is an image-measurement inventory, not a
representative worldwide flowering census. The 512 earlier subset must not
be counted again, and the ongoing complete 4096 fourth wave must later
replace this seven-shard subset rather than be summed with it.

The workflow checks previous source checksums, complete worker receipts,
the original 309,439-photo row prefix, exact identity of all previous
512 photo/source observations, full earlier 310,207 combined photo data,
unique new photo IDs, the disjoint separately targeted 256 arm, and
date/colour consistency. It then recomputes **same named taxon ×0.25-degree
cell×meteorological season-year photographic observations in local summer
and winter**, separately for baseline, ordinary, targeted and pooled audit
source arms. The targeted 256 pixels previously increased groups with
at least one observation on each side from 74 to 141, but groups with
at least two distinct observations in both seasons from just 2 to 3.
Actual seven-shard photo matching is to be evaluated from the signed
result rather than guessed.

**Scientific boundaries:** Original automatic ROI-v4 hue is NOT botanically
verified open petals, photo IDs are not distinct individual plants, and
the 0.25-degree cells are not a field population. Unequal site-season-year
target recruitment must not be used for unbiased global white prevalence
or the main genus-equal original moisture coefficient. WorldClim 1970–2000
annual normals do not measure actual observation-year/month temperature,
irradiance, drought, or fitness. FCP white/darkness/50-km within-species
estimators remain separate. The published-paper target is a global
photo-hue geography and season photo-composition result, NOT yet evidence
of flower-colour plasticity or selection.
