**Central ecological question:** White and chromatic visible flower-like
photographic hues are both widespread globally; their **near-half global
descriptive ratio** is not evolutionarily locked to exactly 50:50. Water,
sunlight and heat may alter their relative frequencies, while pigment costs,
UV signalling, pollinator interactions and reproductive fitness are
alternative biochemical/evolutionary hypotheses that require separate
measurements. Historical annual climate associations are not experimental
flower-pigment fitness effects. Different families, source recruitment and
classifier visibility must not be confused with globally representative
plant abundance. Seasonal observed-month climate belongs to SI and does not
require same-individual floral-colour turnover.
