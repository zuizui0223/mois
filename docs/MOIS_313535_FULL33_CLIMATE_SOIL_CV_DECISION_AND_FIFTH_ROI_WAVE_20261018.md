# MOIS strongest abiotic predictor and future genuine-image acquisition: factual status

**18 October 2026.** The most comprehensive source-verified global
original photo feature lake comprises **313,535 genuine image-pixel
attempts, 151,239 classifiable photographed visible four-hue ROIs,
71,427 white**. A near-half white vs chromatic ratio is not a maintained
evolutionary equilibrium.

**What did the full 33-variable study find?** On 30,109 all-33
complete-case photographed ROIs, temperature had the highest
full-minus-drop held-out predictive importance among five groups
under both whole-genus and broad-geography tests. The absolute
incremental crossvalidated R² was only +0.000946/+0.001255,
respectively. PCA's PC1 explains 30.55% of **environmental**
variation, not flower colour variation.

**Crucial qualification:** Without requiring observed soil,
the climate source includes 151,107 classifiable ROIs, and
moisture ranks top for previously unseen genera while
temperature ranks top for previously unseen broad regions.
Including DEM terrain but not soil changes the source to 131,765
classifiable ROIs and temperature ranks top for both holdouts.
Restricting to the 30,112 soil+terrain-complete source ROIs
but using only climate predictors gives temperature as genus
winner and moisture as regional winner. **No one single strongest
source-generalizable abiotic predictor is established.**
