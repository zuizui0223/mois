## Ecology and data provenance

After the final exact source join, calculate separately the Southern/Northern *photo-white among automatic classifiable ROIs*, *photo four-hue classifiability among all date-bearing original photo attempts*, and *same 0.25°, 0.5°, 1°, 2° source cell×meteorological season-year matched photographic community* summer/winter photo-white difference. Equal site-year contrasts get exploratory **999 geographically clustered** bootstrap percentile intervals, without claiming familywise correction for geographically explored cell scales. The photo frequency of taxonomic identities in summer vs winter is decomposed exactly into within-common-taxon photo-label rates, changing source photographic taxon frequencies and season-exclusive photographed taxa, not biological absence.

Crucially, an iNaturalist current/public observed-on day and an automatic ROI white label do not independently verify an **open petal/tepal stage**. Photos can show bracts, fruit, cropped flowers or technical colour-unknown objects; taxon source presence is not effort-standardized flowering population abundance. Actual **observation-year monthly sunlight/temperature/moisture/VPD** is not identified by WorldClim climatological normals or by observation date alone. The historical MOIS annual genus-equal six-anchor 999-genus maxT water model and FCP within-species W/D/50-km local polymorphism are **distinct estimates**, never silently refit or imported with this date recovery.
