## Exact hemispheric summer-versus-winter photographic white decomposition

Summer means JJA in the north and DJF in the south; winter is the converse.
All cells use the source's actual observation dates, not WorldClim normals.
December belongs to the next local meteorological season-year. Seasons
outside the extratropics are omitted. The raw unpaired source-photograph
difference is exactly reconstructed by four terms (symmetric Kitagawa
within common taxa plus relative source-photograph frequency among shared
taxa, with summer-only and winter-only photo taxon supports algebraically
reintroduced).

All values are **summer-minus-winter WHITE-labelled original-photo
percentage points**, NOT measured floral plasticity.

| Original-photo mixture component | Northern extratropics | Southern extratropics |
|---|---:|---:|
| Within taxa photographed both seasons (photo-label rate term) | +3.26036 | +3.57058 |
| Relative photographic frequency of common taxa | −0.85749 | **+8.03618** |
| Taxa with classifiable photos in summer only | +2.92067 | +4.15779 |
| Taxa with classifiable photos in winter only | −2.49426 | −5.26831 |
| **Source-photo summer-minus-winter total** | **+2.82928** | **+10.49624** |

Northern summer has **47,282** four-hue classifiable photos (49.3528%
white); northern winter **5,077** (46.5235% white). Southern summer has
**7,796** classifiable photos (50.7440% white); southern winter **5,409**
(40.2477% white). Therefore, the large *unpaired* Southern summer-white
excess is predominantly a difference in which photographed shared taxa
receive greater relative photographic weight, with offsetting exclusive
taxon-support terms. **This cannot be interpreted as an 8.04-pp causal
phenological component**, let alone pigment plasticity; taxon photographic
frequency also reflects observer effort, recruitment and visible flower
photograph opportunity.

Most striking: **the two common-taxon terms are unchanged** between the
317,631, 334,015 and 350,399 source frames (North +3.26036 and −0.85749;
South +3.57058 and +8.03618). More real photo pixels, specifically
species-first single-photo acquisitions, do expand geographic/taxonomic
breadth without adding the needed same-taxon *seasonally repeated*
photographic support. The unpaired total varies through summer-only and
winter-only source-taxonomic support; adding >32,000 pixels to the
317,631 core is NOT independent evidence that individual plant pigment
changes across seasons.

## Local and calendar-year identifiability

Within an original named taxon × 0.25-degree geographic cell × same
meteorological season-year:

| Requirement | Northern matched pairs | Southern matched pairs |
|---|---:|---:|
| ≥1 independent source observation per season | 41 | 36 |
| ≥2 independent source observations per season | **2** | **2** |

The ≥2 comparison includes just eight total source observations in each
hemisphere (16 overall). The 0.25-degree source cell may still cover
tens of kilometres. The experiment needed to identify a physiological
within-population white-rate response to *actual observed-year monthly
radiation, precipitation, temperature or VPD* does not exist in this
sample; a lower-pixel but purposefully repeated, verified-open-flower
sampling design is more informative for that question.

## Manuscript and next acquisition decision

1. **Primary MOIS estimand:** global among-taxon photography-derived
four-hue/white composition with independent long-term water/heat/light,
biogeographic and geographic source controls. The source-weighting and
missing-colour sensitivity are mandatory; causal pigment-cost tradeoffs are
untested.
2. **Seasonal SI:** report the exact four-term photographic source identity
and source support. The observed Southern summer excess is a reproducible
photographic assembly pattern, not a causal year-of-observation weather
slope or inherited pigment reversal.
3. **Future targeted seasonal sampling:** acquire already photographed
same taxon/cell/year in opposing seasons, ensure multiple independent
observations per season and independent human review of open petals.
One-per-new-species sampling is for biodiversity breadth, not this estimand.
4. **Full upstream frame:** the separate [93,209,351 candidate-photo-ID
index](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mois/actions/runs/39202507921)
is still distinct from measured photo RGB. Only after its complete signed
Release exists may the observation-probability first wave genuinely
sample new image pixels; source IDs and photo counts alone are never
automatically classified hues.

*No source photo bytes were redistributed. Source status, collector/observer
nonrandomness, flowering-stage image correctness, interspecific phylogeny,
genetics, and fitness remain explicit inferential limits.*
