- [Exact 2026-09-27 iNaturalist 1,000-taxon photo-ID census](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fcp/actions/runs/38884223530): 3,430,502 licensed/validated source photo IDs. - [Observed-on date census](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fcp/actions/runs/38901290387): all 3,430,502 source photos have a valid dated observation; 2,416,225 have positional accuracy >0 and <=5km; 554 taxa have >=1 fine 0.25° site/year with >=2 separately observer-supported calendar months. - [Source-only broad/narrow photographed geographies](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fcp/actions/runs/38901675312): 571 broad, 169 narrow, 259 intermediate, one insufficient; 169 matched pre-pixel pairs. This is not verified native range.
- [First real September 27 original ROI pixel unit](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fcp/actions/runs/38901816890): 64 newly measured original photos from *Cyperus glomeratus* and *Leptomeria drupacea*. Original ROI four-state classifications: 27; after separately flagging same ROI white+yellow organ mixture, 8 white, 15 chromatic and 4 organ mixed; remaining 37 unclassifiable. **ROI-assigned colours have not yet been independently confirmed to represent open flower organs**. No prevalence or within-species seasonal rate inferred. Original images were opened, but original full 3.43M pixels are NOT fully measured.

## Prospective season-matched source sampling expansion, no colour selection

Existing source-only freeze selected 106,376 images with observer-disjoint random photo inclusion, **not optimized for seasonally matched flowering comparisons**. The source population itself has 18,563 temperate site×season-years across 409 taxa with two different seasons and >=2 distinct observations and observers per supported calendar month; original 106,376 selected pool preserves only 215 such site×years across 131 taxa. These are NOT flowering observations nor a randomized estimate of biological seasonality.

Independently reanalysing the signed original full source 0.25° site-month ledger, restrict to no later than independently available TerraClimate 2025, with two LOCAL temperate seasons within the same year/0.25° source geocell, each with at least 24 original independent observations from month records having >=2 observations and >=2 observers: **268 high-depth site×season-years in 39 taxa**. This is an *availability ranking for a separate follow-up PHOTO wave*, not a new taxon prevalence denominator and not a biological flower-colour selection. Actual observer-disjointness across seasons, photo-level flowering/fecundity stage, and original photo/observation identity must be reverified before selecting photos.

Future photo wave requirements:
1. Keep original 1,000-taxon RGFCA repeated random sample and 106,376-image frozen photo-ID selection intact; supplementary seasonal priority never overwrites/retroactively reweights it.
2. Choose two calendar-month/season strata from one taxon and a source 0.25° geocell/year (original source positional accuracy >0 to <=5km), then select independent original observations / independent observers across both seasons using a seed/sha-locked outcome-blind photo-ID hash. A minimum of 24 observations per season is a **resource priority**, not a biological sample-size/power guarantee.
3. Retain ALL full original source ID+date+classifiability denominators and both sampled/unsampled photo IDs; no replacements based on image colour, photo flowering outcome or purported climate effects.
4. **Flowering status is distinct from four-state ROI classification.** The official iNaturalist Open Dataset contains observations/photos/taxa/observers but not a plant phenology annotation column. Observation-level optional iNaturalist 'Flowering / Fruiting / Flower Budding / No Evidence' annotations can be a separate validation signal, but missing annotation is UNKNOWN; an annotation may refer to another photo of the same observation. Verify that the specific ROI is of an open flower rather than a fruit/spikelet/bract or background before the primary white-rate denominator.
5. Post-blind join source observation date + validated monthly climate for tmin,tmax,solar radiation,precipitation,deficit,VPD; do not backfill observations from 2026 with a 2025 monthly value.
6. Reproduce source/year/site and observer/ROI missingness controls before claiming any environmental association. This is observational photography, not genotype frequencies, fitness/costs, evolutionary adaptation or site-level causal selection.
