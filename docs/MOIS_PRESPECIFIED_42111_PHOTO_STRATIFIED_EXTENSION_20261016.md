# Mois — prospective 42,111-taxon stratified photo remeasurement (v1)

**Prespecified 16 October 2026. This document and its code are NOT a newly measured flower-colour result.** Scope: worldwide **flower colour only**, for the single interspecific flower-colour × moisture/geography/soil Mois paper. FCP's separate within-species W/H2 and its uncapped full-photo census remain unchanged.

## Source universe, admission and workload

The pinned iNaturalist 2026-09-27 metadata source contains **42,111 named taxa**, **42,095 with ≥1 photo opportunity**, **16 without**, and **93,209,351 source photo opportunities**. Verified source counts are not 93M measured floral colours or a sealed complete photo-ID index. Source audits: FCP PR #200, Mois source audit of 16 October. The sampler accepts only a genuine post-census **complete original photo-ID reconciliation receipt** having status REAL_ALL_42111_ORIGINAL_PHOTO_ID_COUNTS_RECONCILED_NO_PIXELS. It verifies the full photo-ID ledger fingerprint, original pinned count ledger, 42,111 taxa and 93,209,351 IDs. **Counts-only audits cannot authorize sampling.** Complete the upstream ID freeze on persistent storage without re-downloading the ~36GB metadata archive if the byte-verified original remains available.

For Mois **measurement only**, the cap is **40 independent original observations per taxon, one chosen photo per observation**. The theoretical upper bound is **1,684,440 photos** (1.81% of original 93,209,351 photo opportunities; 16.75× the old 100,543-photo historical source). Actual sample size requires the complete source-ID/observation manifest. Do not imply FCP has a biological 40-photo cap. The old 100,543-source/45,953-classifiable hue findings and original 16,801-species soil36 results retain their historical provenance.

## Outcome-blind selection algorithm

Prepare a **private CSV** with exactly one row per full-source original photo ID, sorted by ascending numeric taxon ID. Required fields are inat_taxon_id, genus, photo_id, observation_id, latitude, longitude, bio12_mm, photo_license. The genus must match the authoritative taxon, and BIO12 comes from the **original observation site**, not from species averages, nearest Mois photos, or observed image colour. Preserve missing site coordinates/precipitation as explicit missing strata. Do not include any flower colour, ROI outcome, white/coloured state, or pigmentation classification field. Original observation IDs, licence identifiers and geoprivacy-sensitive coordinates stay in restricted storage.

1. Within each distinct original **observation**, randomly prioritize original photos by a fixed SHA256 seed and select one representative photo. If the source observation had m photos, any one photo's first-stage inclusion probability is 1/m. Repeated photos of one observation are not independent field visits. Disagreement among their site/precipitation bins fails the run.
2. Define **10 geographic groups**: south extratropical (below −23.5°), tropical, north extratropical (above +23.5°), each crossed with three 120° longitude sectors, plus a missing-coordinate group. Cross these with four annual BIO12 groups: under 500 mm, 500 to under 1500 mm, at least 1500 mm, and missing. There are at most **40 strata per species**.
3. Take at least **one observation from every nonempty stratum**. Allocate remaining places up to the fixed 40/species cap by a prespecified sqrt(observation-opportunity-count) rule. In each stratum, rank observations by a second fixed-seed SHA256 priority; sample without replacement. If fewer than 40 distinct observations exist, include them all.
4. Prior to outcome opening, designate **10% of taxa by a taxon-ID hash** as a technical pilot. The pilot is for access/licensing/segmentation/QC throughput, not ecological colour hypothesis testing. Never exclude later taxa or replace failed photos based on pilot colour labels.

Each sampled observation carries its available source count N_h and selected count k_h. The observation inclusion probability is k_h/N_h. For a selected photo from m photos within its observation, the photo inclusion probability is (k_h/N_h)/m; its photo-population expansion weight is m*N_h/k_h. Retain **both** photo and independent-observation weights: these are different estimands. The sampler refuses forged/incomplete source counts, missing taxa, inconsistent genus/site metadata, duplicate IDs within a taxon, non-increasing taxon blocks, missing photo rights and any colour-leaking columns. It records no colour and no image pixels.

## One-paper bridge to the already verified 13 variables

For the genuinely selected original pixels, use byte-identical historic ROI-v4 + EfficientSAM, fixed nine-palette/four-hue rules and photo-quality gates. Preserve permanent download failure, no flower, failed mask, palette ambiguity and classifiable flower as **different** terminal statuses. Do not infer unknown colour as white or coloured, or substitute a photo after seeing a colour label. Audit original 100,543 ID overlap; a claimed new-photo independent sensitivity must explicitly exclude photo-ID overlaps. Include observer clustering and blinded manually reviewed image-calibration across source strata.

Join the same **13 continuous photo-site predictors** as the verified historical soil36 analysis: geography (absolute latitude, sine/cosine longitude; 3), moisture (BIO12, mean atmospheric vapour pressure; 2), temperature/light (BIO5, annual solar radiation; 2), point **elevation** (1), soil pH, organic carbon, nitrogen, clay and modelled available-water **proxy** (5). The sampling BIO12 category does not replace continuous original-site BIO12 or the other 12 variables. Do not claim DEM slope/TPI from point elevation.

Three publication estimands must be kept separate: (A) classifiability/flower-detection across the photo-opportunity population, (B) design-weighted **classifiable** species-level photo-white share with finite-photo uncertainty, (C) global pooled, genus-between and within-genus regression of photographed white against the same 13 predictors. Report genus-equal **and** species-equal conventions, with 999 whole-genus bootstrap intervals, within-13-variable familywise intervals, source-photo missing-not-at-random bounds, strict-photo sensitivity, and whole-genus and geographic held-out prediction. The image sample never turns photographic phenotypes into genetic morph frequency or pigment-fitness selection.

**Source-verified older results, not new sample results:** in 16,801 taxa /1,665 genera, pooled all-13 white R2 = **2.000% genus-equal**, **1.282% species-equal**; within-genus remaining-white R2 = **0.6202%**; pooled annual BIO12 conditional slope = **+3.770 pp/source SD**, within-13 maxT95 **[+0.817,+6.723]**. It is scientifically valid if a larger, prespecified sample contradicts these estimates. Do not change earlier denominators or silently treat these source-replayed numbers as fresh photo measurements.

## Direct, storage-bounded FCP source-to-Mois bridge (implemented, not yet run on full source)

The first sampler implementation accepts a precomputed, sorted, per-photo original-site BIO12 CSV. A more efficient and safer **direct source-to-selection** implementation now exists at scripts/acquisition/mois_sample_direct_from_fcp_source_20261016.py. It reads the original FCP 128-shard per-taxon photo_manifest.ndjson source directly, authenticates **every original taxon's checkpoint, byte SHA256 and photo count including zero-photo taxa**, and computes the local-site BIO12 group as each original observation is streamed. It does not create a second 93,209,351-row climate-annotated CSV on disk. Its memory use is bounded by the most highly photographed source species plus one small global raster, rather than all species or all original photos at once.

Production input gates are strict: genuine complete source-ID reconciliation; original dated source counts; the immutable per-taxon source hash ledger; a global EPSG:4326, north-up, small WorldClim BIO12 GeoTIFF with a known SHA256; **at least ten held original source-site BIO12 calibration points across at least three geographic strata agreeing within 1mm**; and original source photo licences/URLs/site fields. It refuses source-size or schema drift, missing/duplicated photo IDs within taxa, inconsistent site coordinates per observation, colour/outcome-derived photo replacement, unpinned climate rasters and modified original manifests. Missing raster cells remain missing climate strata rather than being coded as dry conditions.

On a private, adequately provisioned persistent machine **only after FCP completes all-ID source admission**, the direct CLI arguments are:

- --fcp-source-root: private /persistent/fcp/complete_42111_metadata_source containing all finalized 128 per-species source shards
- --complete-counts: private all_42111_species_source_photo_counts.csv
- --source-admission: private independent_original_all_ID_source_admission.json
- --bio12-geotiff and --bio12-sha256: the byte-pinned original WorldClim BIO12 WGS84 raster
- --archived-site-calibration and --archived-site-calibration-sha256: a private source-archived reference CSV with latitude,longitude,bio12_mm
- --out-private-photo-csv and --out-aggregate-receipt: new paths; never overwrite or publish original image URLs/observation coordinates

Both historical and new BIO12 sampling methods are explicitly calibration-gated. If independent original-site values do not match, investigate raster version, resolution, extraction and units; do not silently report comparable 13-variable coefficients.

A subsequent **sample-only** source handoff tool, scripts/acquisition/mois_build_sample_roi_handoff_20261016.py, accepts this actual sampled photo CSV and its SHA256-bound receipt. It creates **128 location-blind worker packet CSVs** with only measurement_id, image_filename and photo_license, **128 private acquisition packet CSVs** containing the original provider URL, and one private identity/environment/selection-probability join key. The measurement_id hashing algorithm matches original FCP ROI-v4 worker identities. Its sealed receipt asserts that this is only the **Mois probabilistic subset**, never a claim that all FCP 93 million original images have been measured. The FCP full-census admission and worker must **NOT** be invoked with these subset packets; separately authorize a sample-scoped executor with original ROI-v4 model-byte verification, bounded source rate and full terminal/measurement reassembly. No such full real-pixel subset run has yet been performed.

Synthetic-only guard tests are tests/test_mois_direct_fcp_source_sampling_20261016.py and tests/test_mois_sample_roi_handoff_20261016.py, in addition to tests/test_mois_stratified_photo_selection_20261016.py. GitHub CI demonstrates code/source contract behavior, not successful original-photo classification or 13-variable ecological inference.
